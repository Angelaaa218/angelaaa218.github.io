Welcome!

I’m Jingyuan Yang (杨静远). I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Management Science and Operations Management at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. I am honored to be advised by Prof. Linwei Xin.

My research focuses on AI for supply chains, with a particular focus on modern inventory management. My recent work explores how foundation models can move beyond forecasting toward decision-making. I am interested in combining the capabilities of foundation models with the structural insight of classical inventory theory to make inventory decisions high-performing, scalable, and implementable in practice, while also providing actionable managerial insights. My research integrates stochastic modeling, optimization, and deep learning.

Before joining Chicago Booth, I earned master’s degrees from Tsinghua University and Columbia University, and a bachelor’s degree from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.
