Welcome!

I’m Jingyuan Yang (杨静远). I am a Ph.D. student in Management Science and Operations Management at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business. I am honored to be advised by Prof. Linwei Xin.

My research focuses on AI for supply chains, especially inventory management. My recent work explores how foundation models can move from forecasting to decision-making. I am interested in combining them with the structural insight of classical inventory theory to make inventory decisions high-performing, scalable, and implementable in practice, while also providing managerial insights. Involved methodologies include stochastic modeling, optimization, and deep learning.

Before joining Chicago Booth, I earned master’s degrees from Tsinghua University and Columbia University, and a bachelor’s degree from Shanghai University of Finance and Economics.
